Research

Things I’m working on across evolutionary genomics, population genetics, and host–parasite evolution.

Boots Lab · UC Berkeley

Host–parasite evolution

Simulation and agent-based modeling of host–parasite dynamics under fast evolution, alongside genomic work on phage generalism–specialism and virus-resistance trade-offs in Plodia interpunctella.

Sudmant Lab · UC Berkeley

Comparative genomics

Comparative genomics of longevity in Myotis bats and the repeated evolution of migratory behavior in Lepidoptera.

Sudmant Lab · UC Berkeley

Fish genome evolution

Population genetics of hybridizing California catostomids and genome evolution in tetraploid catostomids, with a focus on the evolutionary fate of duplicated genes.

Methods

Computation across scales

Agent-based simulation, statistical learning, population genetics, comparative genomics, and software development in R, C++, and Python.
