Research experience

2022–present
Boots Lab, Department of Integrative Biology, UC Berkeley
Advisor: Michael Boots
  • Conducting simulations and agent-based modeling of host–parasite dynamics under a fast-evolution regime.
  • Identifying genomic factors and patterns related to the generalism–specialism continuum in phages.
  • Characterizing genomic variation in Plodia interpunctella to understand the genetic basis of trade-offs in virus resistance.
2022–present
Sudmant Lab, Department of Integrative Biology, UC Berkeley
Advisor: Peter Sudmant
  • Comparative genomics in Myotis bats to understand the genetic basis of increased longevity.
  • Comparative genomics in Lepidoptera to understand the genetic basis for repeated evolution of migratory behavior.
  • Population genetics in hybridizing catostomid species in California.
  • Genome evolution in tetraploid catostomids and the evolutionary fate of gene duplications.
2018–2021
Blackmon Lab, Department of Biology, Texas A&M University
Advisor: Heath Blackmon
  • Development and implementation of tools for analyzing microsatellites in large-scale genomic data, using R and C++.
  • Broad-scale analyses of microsatellites across hundreds of insect genomes.
  • Development of statistical methodology for analyzing retrogene content and evolution in genomes.
2020
Huang Lab, Department of Statistics, Texas A&M University
Advisor: Jianhua Huang
  • Review of tools and algorithms for conducting and evaluating manifold alignment in scRNA-seq data.
  • Constructed software implementation of tools for manifold alignment without correspondence.
  • Constructed analysis pipeline and tutorial for batch correction in Python.

Department of Mathematics, Blinn College
